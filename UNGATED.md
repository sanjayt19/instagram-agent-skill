# Ungated mode

A trust-first month: the account publishes pure value and asks for nothing.
No brand mentions, no offers, no pricing, no product CTAs, no keyword funnels,
no follow-bait. Free resources only, zero gating (no email capture, no signup
walls). Trust and audience first; selling comes later.

When ungated mode is ON, it overrides the pack's CTA rules as follows.
Everything else in each skill stays the same.

## ig-carousel

- The last slide is the **payoff, not a CTA**. End on pure value: a checklist,
  a script, a before/after, a downloadable guide.
- No "comment KEYWORD" slide, no follow ask, no brand footer on any slide.
- The recap (screenshot) slide stays; it is value, not a funnel.

## ig-caption

- The pack's "one ask" rule is **suspended**. No ask at all.
- No keyword, no "link in bio", no follow-bait, no hashtags that are really
  CTAs.
- The caption ends on the thought. Job A and Job B both apply; neither gets
  an ask.

## ig-reel

- The last 3 seconds deliver the payoff, then stop. No ask.
- The loop-back line stays. It is craft, not a CTA.

## ig-story

- Question boxes and polls are fine: conversation, not funnel.
- No keyword boxes, no DM-gated delivery, no "reply for the link".

## ig-dm

- No keyword-triggered posts go out during ungated month, so there is nothing
  to deliver. Draft the copy anyway; it ships when the month ends.

## ig-profile

- The link goes to a free resource, not a product page, while ungated mode
  is on.
